TITLE: FYP Presentation and Defense Guide

PRESENTATION OVERVIEW
FYP presentations are a critical component of your assessment. You will need to present your work to a panel of evaluators and demonstrate your system.

TYPES OF PRESENTATIONS

1. Proposal Presentation (FYP1)
- Duration: 10-15 minutes + Q&A
- Focus: Problem, objectives, methodology, timeline
- Goal: Convince the panel your project is feasible and worthwhile

2. Final Presentation / Viva Voce (FYP2)
- Duration: 15-20 minutes + Q&A
- Focus: Entire project from problem to evaluation
- Goal: Demonstrate your work and defend your approach

PRESENTATION STRUCTURE

For Proposal Presentation:
1. Title slide (1 slide)
2. Introduction and background (1-2 slides)
3. Problem statement (1 slide)
4. Objectives (1 slide)
5. Literature review summary (2-3 slides)
6. Proposed methodology (2-3 slides)
7. System design overview (1-2 slides)
8. Timeline (1 slide)
9. Expected outcomes (1 slide)
10. Questions slide

For Final Presentation:
1. Title slide (1 slide)
2. Introduction and problem (1-2 slides)
3. Objectives (1 slide)
4. Literature review highlights (1-2 slides)
5. Methodology (1-2 slides)
6. System design/architecture (2-3 slides)
7. Implementation highlights (3-4 slides with screenshots)
8. System demo (live or recorded)
9. Testing and evaluation results (2-3 slides)
10. Conclusion and future work (1-2 slides)
11. Questions slide

PRESENTATION TIPS
- Practice multiple times before the actual presentation
- Time yourself to stay within limits
- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- Make eye contact with the panel
- Don't read directly from slides
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs on slides
- Include visually appealing diagrams and screenshots
- Prepare for common questions

DEMO PREPARATION
- Have a backup plan (recorded video) in case of technical issues
- Prepare test data that showcases your system's capabilities
- Know your system inside out
- Be ready to show specific features when asked
- Test all equipment before the presentation

COMMON QUESTIONS FROM PANEL
- Why did you choose this topic?
- How is your project different from existing solutions?
- Why did you choose this particular technology/methodology?
- What were the biggest challenges?
- How did you test your system?
- What are the limitations of your project?
- What would you do differently if you started again?
- How can this project be extended in the future?

HANDLING Q&A
- Listen carefully to the question
- Take a moment to think before answering
- Be honest if you don't know something
- Refer to your report or specific results when possible
- Stay calm and professional
- Thank the panel for their questions
