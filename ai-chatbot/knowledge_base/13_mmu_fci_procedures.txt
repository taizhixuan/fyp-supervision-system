TITLE: MMU FCI FYP Procedures and Policies

DISCLAIMER: This document provides general guidance about typical FYP procedures at MMU FCI. Always check with the FYP committee for the latest official procedures, deadlines, and requirements.

GENERAL FYP REGISTRATION
- Students register for FYP as part of their course enrollment
- FYP1 is typically taken in the penultimate semester
- FYP2 is taken in the final semester
- Prerequisites may apply (check with your programme coordinator)

SUPERVISOR ASSIGNMENT PROCESS
1. Students submit supervision requests through the system
2. Students can indicate their preferred supervisors
3. Supervisors review and accept/reject requests
4. The FYP committee may reassign students if needed
5. Once confirmed, the supervisor-student pair is formalized

PROPOSAL SUBMISSION AND REVIEW
1. Students write proposals with supervisor guidance
2. Proposals are submitted through the official system
3. The FYP committee reviews proposals
4. Proposals may be approved, require revision, or be rejected
5. Students must address reviewer feedback
6. Final proposal approval is required before proceeding to implementation

PROGRESS MONITORING
- Regular supervision meetings are mandatory
- Meeting logs must be submitted and signed
- Mid-semester reviews may be conducted
- Students must show continuous progress
- Failure to meet with supervisor may affect assessment

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
- All work must be original
- Plagiarism is a serious academic offense
- Cite all sources properly
- Use plagiarism checking tools before submission
- Code written by others must be attributed
- Using AI tools: check your programme's specific policy

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
- Follow the official format and template
- Submit both soft copy and hard copy (if required)
- Meet all submission deadlines
- Include all required documents (report, meeting logs, source code)
- Ensure proper binding and formatting (for hard copy)

ASSESSMENT CRITERIA
Typical assessment components:
- Proposal and presentation (FYP1)
- Supervisor evaluation
- Examiner evaluation
- Final report quality
- Final presentation and demo
- Meeting log completeness
- Technical complexity and innovation

CONTACT INFORMATION
For FYP-related queries:
- Check the FYP committee notice board
- Email the FYP coordinator
- Attend FYP briefing sessions
- Check the official university portal for announcements

Note: Specific policies, deadlines, and procedures may vary by programme and semester. Always refer to the official FYP guidelines document provided by your faculty.
